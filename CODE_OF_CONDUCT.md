# Community conduct

Nomily welcomes constructive, technical, and respectful participation.

Be kind, stay on topic, and discuss ideas and evidence rather than people.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, and sharing another
person's recordings, transcripts, credentials, or private data are not
acceptable.

If you experience or observe a problem, contact
[nomily@geekis.com](mailto:nomily@geekis.com). Maintainers may remove content
or restrict participation to protect contributors and users.
